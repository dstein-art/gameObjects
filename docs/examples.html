Example Games

Five complete games built with GameObjects. Each has its own page where you can play the finished game, then build it yourself, step by step. Every step is a complete sketch you can run, change and break.

The tutorials go roughly from easiest to hardest. Each one starts from an empty sketch, so you can do them in any order.

5 steps

Physics Sandbox

Drop, drag and throw shapes. Your first sketch with a floor, a ramp, a group and the mouse.

 5 steps

Joints Playground

A rope bridge, a pendulum and a spring, all held together with joints.

 6 steps

Breakout

A paddle, a ball and a wall of bricks. Your first class that extends GameObject.

 6 steps

Platformer

Run, jump and collect coins across a level bigger than the screen, with a following camera.

 7 steps

Space Shooter

Thrust, shoot and split asteroids, plus an animated rocket flame.

How the tutorials work

  • Each step adds one idea and explains the new code.
  • Each step has a live demo with the complete code so far. Press ▶ to run your changes, or use the copy icon to take the code into the p5.js Web Editor.
  • The last step is the finished game. The "Make it your own" ideas at the end of each page suggest where to take it next.

Tip: the bigger games are shrunk to fit the page. Use the open in a new tab icon to play them at full size.
